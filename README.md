# Healthcare-Insights
# 📊 Healthcare Data Analysis Project

## **Overview**
This project analyzes **hospital admissions, billing trends, and insurance impact** using **SQL & Tableau**. The goal is to provide actionable insights to optimize hospital efficiency and financial performance.

## **Tools Used**
- **SQL** (Data Cleaning & Analysis)
- **Tableau** (Visualization & Dashboards)
- **Excel** (Data Preprocessing)

## **Dataset**
- **Records:** 55,500 patient records
- **Key Fields:** Admission Date, Billing Amount, Medical Condition, Insurance Provider, Discharge Date

## **Key Insights & Findings**
### **1️⃣ Billing Trends & Revenue Analysis**
- **Billing revenue increased by 15% year-over-year**, with peaks in Q3.
- 🔹 **Actionable Insight:** Optimize billing processes and prepare for seasonal surges.

### **2️⃣ Admissions by Medical Condition**
- **Diabetes & heart disease account for 40% of admissions.**
- 🔹 **Actionable Insight:** Allocate more resources to these high-demand conditions.

### **3️⃣ Insurance Provider Impact**
- **Medicare covers 60% of hospital bills**, affecting reimbursement rates.
- 🔹 **Actionable Insight:** Adjust financial strategies based on insurance coverage distribution.
